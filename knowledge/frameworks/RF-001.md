# Enterprise Quantum Readiness Framework

## Overview

The Enterprise Quantum Readiness Framework (EQRF) provides a structured methodology for evaluating an organisation's preparedness for the transition to post-quantum cryptography. Rather than focusing solely on technology, the framework assesses governance, people, processes, architecture, operational capability and organisational readiness to support sustainable quantum-safe transformation.

---

## Why the Framework Matters

Many organisations understand that quantum computing will affect cryptographic security, but very few understand whether they are operationally prepared for migration.

Readiness is therefore not measured by awareness alone. It is measured by an organisation's ability to identify cryptographic assets, establish governance, prioritise migration activities and execute transformation with minimal business disruption.

---

## Core Capability Domains

The framework evaluates readiness across six enterprise domains:

- Governance and Leadership
- Cryptographic Asset Visibility
- Technology and Architecture
- Business Processes
- Workforce Capability
- Risk, Compliance and Assurance

Each domain contributes to an overall organisational readiness profile.

---

## Readiness Levels

The framework defines five progressive maturity levels.

### Level 1 — Initial

Quantum awareness is limited and cryptographic assets are largely unknown.

### Level 2 — Developing

Discovery activities begin and governance structures are being established.

### Level 3 — Managed

Critical cryptographic assets are identified, governance is operational and migration planning is underway.

### Level 4 — Optimised

Migration programmes are integrated into enterprise architecture and supported by continuous governance.

### Level 5 — Quantum Ready

The organisation demonstrates cryptographic agility, continuous monitoring and sustained post-quantum resilience.

---

## Expected Outcomes

Organisations applying the framework should be able to:

- Understand enterprise quantum readiness.
- Identify capability gaps.
- Prioritise migration initiatives.
- Support executive decision-making.
- Improve governance maturity.
- Measure readiness over time.

---

## Relationship to EQMP

Within the Enterprise Quantum Migration Platform, the Enterprise Quantum Readiness Framework provides the assessment model used to evaluate organisational maturity before migration planning begins.

The framework complements the Governance-Aware PQC Migration Framework by establishing the baseline from which migration strategies are developed.
