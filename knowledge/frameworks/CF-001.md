# Compliance & Regulatory Alignment Framework

## Overview

The Compliance & Regulatory Alignment Framework (CRAF) provides a governance-driven methodology for aligning enterprise post-quantum cryptographic migration with regulatory obligations, industry standards and organisational policies. It enables organisations to demonstrate that migration activities are not only technically effective but also compliant, auditable and strategically governed.

---

## Purpose

Post-quantum migration is more than a technology programme. It is a governance initiative that must satisfy legal, regulatory and operational expectations across the enterprise.

This framework establishes a structured approach for integrating compliance into every stage of the migration lifecycle, ensuring that security transformation supports organisational accountability and regulatory assurance.

---

## Governance Principles

The framework is built upon six guiding principles:

- Governance by Design
- Regulatory Alignment
- Risk-Based Decision Making
- Accountability and Ownership
- Continuous Compliance
- Evidence-Based Assurance

These principles ensure that migration activities remain transparent, measurable and defensible throughout their lifecycle.

---

## Compliance Domains

The framework evaluates organisational alignment across key compliance areas, including:

- Cybersecurity Governance
- Data Protection and Privacy
- Risk Management
- Enterprise Architecture
- Cryptographic Policy
- Audit and Assurance
- Third-Party Risk
- Operational Resilience

Each domain contributes to an organisation's overall compliance maturity.

---

## Framework Lifecycle

The framework follows a continuous governance cycle:

1. Identify Regulatory Requirements
2. Assess Current Compliance Posture
3. Map Controls to Migration Activities
4. Implement Governance Controls
5. Monitor Compliance Performance
6. Audit, Report and Improve

This lifecycle supports continuous improvement rather than one-time compliance exercises.

---

## Enterprise Benefits

Applying the framework enables organisations to:

- Align migration with regulatory expectations.
- Strengthen governance oversight.
- Improve audit readiness.
- Demonstrate executive accountability.
- Reduce compliance-related risk.
- Support sustainable post-quantum transformation.

## Relationship to EQMP

Within the Enterprise Quantum Migration Platform, the Compliance & Regulatory Alignment Framework provides the governance layer that connects assessment findings, migration planning and executive reporting with organisational policies and external regulatory requirements.

It ensures that every migration recommendation can be traced to measurable governance and compliance objectives.
