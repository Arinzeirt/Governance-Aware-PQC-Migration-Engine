# Governance-Aware PQC Migration Framework

## Overview

The Governance-Aware Post-Quantum Cryptographic (PQC) Migration Framework provides a structured methodology for planning, governing and executing enterprise-wide migration from classical cryptography to quantum-resistant cryptography.

Rather than treating migration as a technology replacement exercise, the framework positions governance as the central mechanism for managing cryptographic transformation across people, processes, technology and regulatory obligations.

The framework forms one of the foundational research assets of the Enterprise Quantum Migration Platform (EQMP).

---

## Research Problem

Many organisations recognise that quantum computing threatens today's public-key cryptography, yet very few possess an enterprise-wide governance model capable of coordinating migration across business units, technology platforms and regulatory requirements.

Without governance, organisations often experience:

- Incomplete cryptographic inventories
- Inconsistent migration priorities
- Limited executive visibility
- Poor coordination between technical and business stakeholders
- Increased long-term cyber risk

---

## Research Objectives

The framework aims to:

- Establish governance before migration activities begin.
- Create enterprise-wide cryptographic visibility.
- Align migration with business priorities.
- Improve executive decision making.
- Support regulatory compliance.
- Enable long-term cryptographic agility.

---

## Framework Principles

The Governance-Aware PQC Migration Framework is built upon the following principles:

1. Governance before implementation.
2. Enterprise-wide cryptographic visibility.
3. Risk-informed migration planning.
4. Business-driven prioritisation.
5. Continuous measurement and improvement.
6. Executive accountability.

---

## Core Framework Components

The framework currently consists of eight interacting domains:

1. Governance
2. Cryptographic Discovery
3. Asset Inventory
4. Risk Assessment
5. Readiness Assessment
6. Migration Planning
7. Executive Decision Support
8. Continuous Improvement

Each component contributes to an integrated migration lifecycle rather than functioning as an isolated activity.

---

## Expected Outcomes

Organisations implementing this framework should be able to:

- Improve enterprise cryptographic visibility.
- Prioritise migration activities objectively.
- Strengthen governance and oversight.
- Improve regulatory preparedness.
- Reduce long-term quantum-related cyber risk.
- Build sustainable post-quantum resilience.

---

## Relationship to EQMP

The Governance-Aware PQC Migration Framework provides the strategic foundation for the Enterprise Quantum Migration Platform.

Other research assets—including the Enterprise Quantum Readiness Framework, the Migration Decision Engine, the Cryptographic Discovery & Inventory Model and future AI-assisted capabilities—build upon this governance model.
