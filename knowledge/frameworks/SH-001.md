# Self-Healing Cryptographic Resilience Framework

## Overview

The Self-Healing Cryptographic Resilience Framework (SHCRF) is an enterprise resilience model that extends traditional post-quantum migration beyond one-time transformation programmes. It introduces continuous monitoring, governance-aware automation and intelligent decision support to help organisations sustain cryptographic resilience throughout the lifecycle of their digital infrastructure.

Rather than viewing migration as a project with a fixed endpoint, the framework treats quantum readiness as a continuous operational capability that adapts to evolving threats, technologies and business priorities.

---

## Vision

Enterprise security should not depend solely on periodic assessments or manual intervention.

The vision of the Self-Healing Cryptographic Resilience Framework is to enable organisations to continuously evaluate their cryptographic posture, identify emerging risks, recommend appropriate remediation actions and maintain long-term resilience through governance-driven automation.

---

## Guiding Principles

The framework is founded on seven principles:

- Continuous Cryptographic Visibility
- Governance-Aware Automation
- Cryptographic Agility
- Risk-Based Adaptation
- Policy-Driven Decision Making
- AI-Assisted Operational Intelligence
- Continuous Enterprise Learning

Together, these principles establish a resilient operating model capable of responding to changing cryptographic conditions without compromising governance or business continuity.

---

## Core Capability Layers

The framework consists of six integrated capability layers:

### Continuous Monitoring

Continuously observe enterprise cryptographic assets, certificates, protocols and dependencies to detect changes that may affect quantum readiness.

### Threat Intelligence

Integrate emerging cryptographic research, standards updates and quantum threat intelligence into enterprise decision-making.

### Policy & Governance Engine

Evaluate findings against organisational policies, governance controls and regulatory obligations before recommending corrective actions.

### AI Decision Support

Prioritise recommendations using business impact, operational risk, compliance requirements and organisational readiness.

### Automated Remediation

Support policy-approved remediation workflows such as certificate replacement, cryptographic upgrades and migration task orchestration while maintaining executive oversight.

### Continuous Assurance

Measure organisational resilience over time using governance metrics, operational indicators and readiness assessments to demonstrate sustained improvement.

---

## Operational Lifecycle

The framework follows a continuous improvement cycle:

1. Observe
2. Analyse
3. Evaluate
4. Recommend
5. Remediate
6. Validate
7. Learn

Each cycle strengthens enterprise resilience by ensuring that lessons learned from previous activities improve future decision-making.
