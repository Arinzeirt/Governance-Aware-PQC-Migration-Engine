# Purpose

The Roadmap defines the long-term evolution of the Enterprise Quantum Migration Platform (EQMP).

Rather than tracking software releases alone, this document describes how EQMP will mature from a research-driven platform into a globally recognised enterprise solution for Post-Quantum Cryptographic migration.

The roadmap aligns engineering, research and product development around a shared long-term vision.

---

# Platform Evolution

Each stage builds upon the previous stage while maintaining alignment with the Governance-Aware Assessment Methodology.

# Success Vision

The long-term success of EQMP is not measured by the number of software features delivered.

Success is measured by the platform's ability to help organisations achieve Enterprise Quantum Readiness through research, governance, intelligence and strategic migration planning.

The roadmap therefore represents the long-term evolution of EQMP from an enterprise assessment platform into a globally recognised decision intelligence platform for Post-Quantum Cryptographic migration.
