# Purpose

This document defines the long-term product vision of the Enterprise Quantum Migration Platform (EQMP).

It explains what EQMP aims to become, the value it delivers to organisations, the customers it serves, and the strategic direction that guides future product development.

While the architecture defines how the platform is organised, this document defines why the platform exists as a product.

---

# Product Vision

EQMP aims to become the leading enterprise platform for governance-aware post-quantum cryptographic migration.

The platform enables organisations to move beyond awareness by providing the intelligence, governance, assessment and planning capabilities required to prepare for the transition to Post-Quantum Cryptography.

EQMP is designed to become an enterprise decision-support platform rather than a traditional cybersecurity assessment tool.

---

# Mission

To enable organisations worldwide to confidently assess, govern and execute their migration to Post-Quantum Cryptography through research-driven methodologies and enterprise intelligence.

---

# Product Position

EQMP combines multiple capabilities into a single enterprise platform.

It is simultaneously:

- A Research Platform
- A Knowledge Platform
- An Enterprise Assessment Platform
- An Intelligence Platform
- A Governance Platform
- A Reporting Platform
- A Migration Planning Platform

Together these capabilities provide a complete enterprise quantum readiness experience.

# Core Value Proposition

EQMP helps organisations answer critical questions such as:

- Where is our cryptography?
- Which assets are vulnerable?
- How prepared are we for the quantum era?
- Which standards apply?
- Which regulatory obligations are affected?
- What should be migrated first?
- How should migration be governed?
