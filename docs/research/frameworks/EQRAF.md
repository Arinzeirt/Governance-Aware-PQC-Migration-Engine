# Enterprise Quantum Readiness Assessment Framework (EQRAF)

Version: 1.0 (Research Draft)

Platform: Enterprise Quantum Migration Platform (EQMP)

Status: Core Research Framework

---

# Overview

The Enterprise Quantum Readiness Assessment Framework (EQRAF) is the primary assessment methodology within the Enterprise Quantum Migration Platform (EQMP).

EQRAF provides a governance-driven approach for evaluating an organisation's readiness to transition from classical cryptographic systems to post-quantum cryptography.

Unlike traditional cryptographic assessments that focus primarily on algorithms and technical inventories, EQRAF evaluates organisational readiness across governance, enterprise technology, cryptographic posture and strategic transformation.

The framework produces executive-level findings that support enterprise decision-making, migration planning and long-term organisational resilience.

---

# Vision

EQRAF enables organisations to understand where they are today, identify where they need to be tomorrow and establish a structured pathway towards enterprise post-quantum readiness.

---

# Framework Objectives

The framework is designed to:

• Assess enterprise readiness for post-quantum transformation.

• Evaluate organisational capability beyond technical cryptography.

• Provide executive-level insights for strategic planning.

• Support governance-driven migration decisions.

• Generate evidence-based recommendations.

• Establish a repeatable enterprise assessment methodology.

---

# Assessment Philosophy

EQRAF is built upon five principles.

## 1. Governance First

Successful post-quantum migration is an organisational transformation rather than a technology replacement project.

---

## 2. Enterprise Perspective

Assessment considers people, processes, governance, technology and organisational strategy.

---

## 3. Risk-Based Decision Making

Migration priorities are determined by enterprise risk rather than technology alone.

---

## 4. Executive Communication

Assessment results should be understandable by executive leadership as well as technical teams.

---

## 5. Continuous Improvement

Quantum readiness is an evolving organisational capability rather than a one-time assessment.

---

# Assessment Domains

EQRAF evaluates five enterprise domains.

## Domain 1

Enterprise Profile

Purpose

Establish organisational context.

---

## Domain 2

Technology Landscape

Purpose

Understand the enterprise technology environment supporting cryptographic operations.

---

## Domain 3

Cryptographic Posture

Purpose

Evaluate the organisation's current cryptographic exposure and migration complexity.

---

## Domain 4

Governance & Risk

Purpose

Assess organisational capability to govern enterprise-wide cryptographic transformation.

---

## Domain 5

Transformation Readiness

Purpose

Determine overall organisational readiness using evidence collected across the previous four domains.

Transformation Readiness is calculated by the framework rather than directly completed by the organisation.
