# Enterprise Quantum Migration Platform (EQMP)

## Research Library

Welcome to the official research library for the **Enterprise Quantum Migration Platform (EQMP)**.

This repository contains the research methodologies, enterprise frameworks, architectural models, implementation documentation, and future publications supporting the EQMP research programme.

---

# Research Vision

The Enterprise Quantum Migration Platform (EQMP) is a governance-driven enterprise platform designed to support organisations throughout their transition from classical cryptographic systems to post-quantum cryptography.

Rather than focusing solely on cryptographic migration, EQMP addresses enterprise governance, organisational readiness, migration planning, executive decision support, and long-term transformation.

---

# Research Programme

The research programme consists of five primary research contributions.

1. Enterprise Quantum Migration Platform (EQMP)
2. Enterprise Quantum Readiness Assessment Framework (EQRAF)
3. Governance-Aware PQC Migration Framework (GAPMF)
4. Migration Decision Engine (MDE)
5. Enterprise Migration Workspace (EMW)

Together, these contributions establish a governance-driven methodology for enterprise post-quantum transformation.
