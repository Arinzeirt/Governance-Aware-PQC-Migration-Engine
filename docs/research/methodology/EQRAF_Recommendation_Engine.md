# EQRAF Recommendation Engine

Version: 1.0 (Research Draft)

Framework: Enterprise Quantum Readiness Assessment Framework (EQRAF)

Platform: Enterprise Quantum Migration Platform (EQMP)

---

# Purpose

The EQRAF Recommendation Engine transforms enterprise assessment findings into governance-driven strategic recommendations.

Rather than generating generic security advice, the Recommendation Engine produces context-aware recommendations that reflect the organisation's business environment, technology landscape, governance capability and cryptographic posture.

The Recommendation Engine serves as the decision-support component of EQRAF and provides executives with practical guidance for enterprise post-quantum transformation.

---

# Design Principles

The Recommendation Engine follows six guiding principles.

## 1. Evidence Driven

Every recommendation must be supported by one or more assessment findings.

---

## 2. Governance First

Recommendations prioritise governance and organisational capability before technical implementation.

---

## 3. Executive Focus

Recommendations are written for executive decision-makers while remaining actionable for technical teams.

---

## 4. Business Context

Recommendations consider industry, organisational size, critical infrastructure status and enterprise risk.

---

## 5. Prioritised Execution

Recommendations are organised into implementation phases to support structured transformation.

---

## 6. Explainability

Every recommendation should clearly explain why it has been generated.

# Recommendation Categories

The Recommendation Engine organises recommendations into six categories.

## Governance

Examples

- Establish executive sponsorship.
- Form a Post-Quantum Governance Committee.
- Define organisational accountability.
- Develop governance policies.

---

## Technology

Examples

- Improve cryptographic visibility.
- Modernise PKI infrastructure.
- Review enterprise applications.
- Assess cloud cryptographic services.

---

## Cryptography

Examples

- Build a cryptographic inventory.
- Prioritise RSA migration.
- Improve cryptographic agility.
- Review certificate lifecycle management.

---

## Risk Management

Examples

- Identify systems exposed to Harvest Now, Decrypt Later risks.
- Perform enterprise risk assessment.
- Classify critical assets.
- Review third-party dependencies.

---

## Compliance

Examples

- Align migration planning with regulatory obligations.
- Review internal security policies.
- Establish governance reporting.
- Maintain implementation evidence.

---

## Transformation

Examples

- Develop an enterprise migration roadmap.
- Establish phased implementation.
- Monitor organisational readiness.
- Review progress continuously.

# Recommendation Priority Levels

Recommendations are assigned one of three priority levels.

## Immediate

Timeframe

0–90 Days

Purpose

Establish governance and visibility.

Typical Activities

- Governance ownership
- Cryptographic discovery
- Executive awareness
- Asset identification

---

## Medium-Term

Timeframe

3–12 Months

Purpose

Prepare organisational capability.

Typical Activities

- Migration planning
- Vendor engagement
- Technology upgrades
- Policy development

---

## Long-Term

Timeframe

Beyond 12 Months

Purpose

Execute and optimise enterprise transformation.

Typical Activities

- PQC deployment
- Continuous governance
- Readiness reassessment
- Enterprise optimisation

# Recommendation Structure

Every recommendation produced by EQRAF contains five components.

## Title

A concise executive recommendation.

---

## Business Rationale

Why the recommendation matters.

---

## Supporting Findings

Assessment findings that produced the recommendation.

---

## Expected Benefits

The organisational outcomes expected after implementation.

---

## Suggested Timeline

Immediate, Medium-Term or Long-Term.

---

# Transformation Roadmap

Recommendations are combined into an enterprise transformation roadmap.

The roadmap enables organisations to move from their current maturity level toward higher levels of enterprise quantum readiness through structured, phased implementation.

---

# Executive Reporting

Within the Enterprise Quantum Readiness Preview and the Enterprise Quantum Readiness Report, recommendations are presented as strategic actions rather than technical tasks.

This ensures that executive stakeholders understand both the business importance and implementation priorities.
