# EQRAF Decision Logic

Version: 1.0 (Research Draft)

Framework: Enterprise Quantum Readiness Assessment Framework (EQRAF)

Platform: Enterprise Quantum Migration Platform (EQMP)

---

# Purpose

The EQRAF Decision Logic defines how assessment responses are transformed into enterprise findings, maturity assessments and executive recommendations.

Rather than relying on simple pass/fail scoring, EQRAF evaluates organisational evidence collected throughout the assessment and applies governance-driven decision rules to determine enterprise readiness.

The decision logic provides consistency, transparency and repeatability across all assessments.

---

# Decision Philosophy

EQRAF follows five decision principles.

1. Context before scoring.

Assessment findings must consider organisational context rather than isolated technical responses.

---

2. Governance before technology.

Governance capability significantly influences successful enterprise transformation.

---

3. Enterprise risk before technical severity.

Recommendations are prioritised according to organisational impact rather than technical complexity.

---

4. Explainability.

Every recommendation generated by EQRAF must be traceable to one or more assessment findings.

---

5. Executive relevance.

Outputs should support executive decision-making rather than purely technical analysis.

# Finding Generation

EQRAF transforms assessment evidence into executive findings.

Example

Evidence

RSA is used extensively across customer-facing systems.

Finding

The organisation demonstrates significant reliance on classical public-key cryptography requiring structured migration planning.

---

Evidence

No migration strategy exists.

Finding

Formal governance for post-quantum transformation has not yet been established.

---

Evidence

Long-term sensitive information is retained.

Finding

The organisation may be exposed to Harvest Now, Decrypt Later risks and should prioritise cryptographic review.

---

# Recommendation Generation

Each recommendation must be supported by one or more findings.

Example

Finding

Cryptographic inventory is incomplete.

Recommendation

Establish an enterprise-wide cryptographic discovery programme.

---

Finding

Governance committee absent.

Recommendation

Create a cross-functional post-quantum governance committee with executive sponsorship.

---

Finding

Migration strategy unavailable.

Recommendation

Develop a governance-driven enterprise migration roadmap aligned with business priorities.

---

# Recommendation Priority

EQRAF organises recommendations into three planning horizons.

## Immediate

0–90 Days

Examples

- Establish governance ownership.
- Begin cryptographic discovery.
- Identify critical assets.

---

## Medium-Term

3–12 Months

Examples

- Develop migration roadmap.
- Improve cryptographic agility.
- Review third-party dependencies.

---

## Long-Term

Beyond 12 Months

Examples

- Execute phased migration.
- Continuous governance review.
- Enterprise-wide optimisation.
