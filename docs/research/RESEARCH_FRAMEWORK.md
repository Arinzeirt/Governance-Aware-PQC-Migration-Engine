# Purpose

The Research Framework defines how research drives the evolution of the Enterprise Quantum Migration Platform (EQMP).

EQMP is not developed through feature requests alone. Every major platform capability should be supported by research, validated through analysis, and translated into practical enterprise value.

This document establishes the research lifecycle that transforms academic investigation into enterprise-ready capabilities.

---

# Research Philosophy

Research is the foundation of EQMP.

The platform is built on the belief that meaningful innovation begins with understanding a problem before attempting to solve it.

Every major feature should answer three questions:

- What problem does it solve?
- What research supports it?
- What value does it deliver to an enterprise?

Research is therefore not a supporting activity—it is a core engineering discipline within EQMP.

---

# The EQMP Research Lifecycle

Every significant capability should follow the same lifecycle.

```text
Problem

↓

Observation

↓

Research Question

↓

Literature Review

↓

Research

↓

Research Note

↓

Framework

↓

Assessment Methodology

↓

Platform Capability

↓

Enterprise Intelligence

↓

Enterprise Value

↓

Publication

↓

Knowledge Base
```

This lifecycle ensures that new capabilities are evidence-based, repeatable and aligned with the long-term vision of the platform.

# Research Components

The Research Framework consists of several interconnected components.

## Research

Formal investigation into emerging challenges relating to Post-Quantum Cryptography, governance, enterprise architecture and cryptographic migration.

---

## Research Notes

Research Notes capture focused observations, insights and discoveries.

Each Research Note explores a specific problem and provides the foundation for future frameworks and platform capabilities.

Examples include:

- Harvest Now, Decrypt Later
- You Cannot Migrate What You Cannot See
- Unknown Cryptography Is Your Biggest Risk
- Governance Decides Success

---

## Frameworks

Frameworks organise research into structured methodologies.

Frameworks provide repeatable approaches that can be implemented consistently within the platform.

---

## Assessment Methodology

The Governance-Aware Assessment Methodology applies the outputs of research and frameworks to enterprise assessments.

It transforms research into practical decision-making processes.

---

## Platform Capabilities

Validated methodologies are implemented as platform capabilities.

Examples include:

- Technology Discovery
- Cryptographic Discovery
- Governance Assessment
- Risk Assessment
- Compliance Mapping
- Standards Alignment
- Migration Planning

---

## Enterprise Intelligence

Platform capabilities generate enterprise intelligence rather than isolated technical findings.

This intelligence supports executive decision making, governance and strategic planning.

# Relationship with the Platform

Research directly influences every major platform.

Research supports:

- Enterprise Experience Platform
- Research & Knowledge Platform
- Governance-Aware Assessment Methodology
- Assessment Platform
- Intelligence Platform
- Enterprise Reporting Platform

No major capability should be introduced without understanding the research that justifies its existence.

# Guiding Principles

The Research Framework follows these principles:

- Research Before Implementation
- Evidence-Based Engineering
- Continuous Learning
- Governance by Design
- Enterprise Relevance
- Standards Alignment
- Practical Innovation
- Knowledge Sharing

---

# Long-Term Vision

The long-term objective of EQMP is to establish a continuously evolving research ecosystem where new discoveries become frameworks, frameworks become methodologies, methodologies become platform capabilities and platform capabilities improve enterprise quantum readiness.

Research is therefore not the beginning of EQMP.

Research is the engine that continually drives its evolution.
